Privacy Policy

After the account holder authorizes access, Bot Semangat reads public TikTok video IDs and creation times.

This information is used only to check whether a video was uploaded on the current day and send a Discord reminder.

OAuth tokens and limited activity data are stored on the bot owner's private device.

Chat messages sent to the bot may be processed by OpenRouter and its AI providers.

The data is not sold or used for advertising.
